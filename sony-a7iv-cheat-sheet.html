    Start from:
    1 stop = double or half the light.
    Aperture one stop: f/2.8 ↔ f/4 ↔ f/5.6
    Shutter one stop: 1/250 ↔ 1/125 ↔ 1/60
    ISO one stop: 400 ↔ 800 ↔ 1600
    Close aperture 2 stops → slow shutter 2 stops or raise ISO 2 stops.

    Which mode?

    • AUTO — camera decides everything. Use it for your first day.
    • A (aperture priority) — you pick f/, camera picks shutter. Best everyday mode.
    • S (shutter priority) — you pick speed, camera picks f/. Sports, kids, waterfalls.
    • M (manual) — you pick both. Add Auto ISO and it becomes easy.

    Rules to remember

    • Handheld: shutter at least 1/(focal length). 50mm → 1/50 or faster. IBIS buys you 2–3 stops for still subjects.
    • Moving people: 1/250 minimum. Running kids or sport: 1/1000.
    • Too bright or dark? Turn the top-right dial (exposure comp) before touching anything else.
    • Check the histogram: press DISP until it shows. Avoid a pile-up hard against the right edge.

    Focus sharp every time

    Focus mode

    AF-SFocuses once and locks. Still subjects, landscapes.
    AF-CKeeps refocusing. People, pets, anything moving. Default choice.
    DMFAutofocus, then fine-tune with the lens ring.
    MFManual. Stars, fireworks, through glass.

    Focus area

    WideCamera picks. Works well with subject recognition.
    ZoneYou pick a region, camera picks within it.
    Spot S/M/LExactly where you point with the joystick.
    TrackingPick a subject, it follows them round the frame.

    Handy tricks

    • Focus magnifier: assign to a C button to zoom in and check manual focus.
    • Peaking: Focus → Peaking Display outlines what's sharp in colour when focusing manually.
    • Touch focus: tap the screen to pick a subject; it tracks from there.
    • Back-button focus: set AF w/ Shutter to Off, then focus with AF-ON only.
    • Video: turn on Breathing Compensation so the frame doesn't "zoom" while focus shifts.

    Looks & profiles

    • Creative Look (Exposure/Color → Color/Tone): ST standard, PT portrait, VV / VV2 vivid, FL film, IN matte, SH soft, BW black & white.
    • S-Cinetone (Picture Profile PP11): cinematic video colour straight out of camera. No editing needed.
    • S-Log3 (PP8/PP9): flat, for colour grading. Expose +1 to +1.7 brighter than normal and always edit it.
    • Custom WB: fill the frame with a white paper under your light and set Custom 1 for perfect colour.

    Exposure in bright sun

    At 1/50 outdoors you can't close the aperture enough without losing the blurry background. Use a variable ND filter (ND2–ND400) to keep 1/50 at f/2.8.

    Audio

    • Built-in mic is only for reference; use a shotgun or wireless mic in the Multi-Interface shoe or mic jack.
    • Shooting → Audio Recording → Audio Rec Level: peaks around −12 dB.
    • Plug headphones in to monitor; turn on Wind Noise Reduct. outdoors.

    Checks before you press record

    • Switch set to Movie, mode M or S.
    • Shutter on 1/50 or 1/100, WB set manually.
    • Zebras on at 70 (skin) or 100+ (highlights): Exposure/Color → Zebra Display.
    • Plenty of card space and battery. Recording can run long in 4K 25p.

    Stop flicker & banding 50 Hz mains

    Indoor LED and tube lights pulse 100 times a second. Match your shutter speed to it.

    Troubleshooting quick fixes

    ProblemLikely causeFix
    Photos blurryShutter too slow, or focused on the wrong thingFaster shutter (1/250 for people). Check the green focus box is on the subject.
    Whole photo too dark / brightMetering fooled by a bright or dark sceneTurn the exposure compensation dial. Check the histogram.
    Grainy photosISO very highAdd light, use a wider aperture (lower f/), or accept a slower shutter.
    Dark bands across photoElectronic shutter under LED lightsTurn Silent Mode off, turn Anti-flicker on.
    Video flickersNTSC frame rate or wrong shutterPAL, 25p/50p, shutter 1/50 or 1/100. See Flicker.
    Shutter won't fireNo card, AF-S can't focus, or buffer fullInsert card, switch to AF-C or MF, wait for the card-write light.
    Can't change apertureWrong mode, or lens has an aperture ring not on AUse A or M. Set the lens ring to "A".
    Screen goes black at your eyeEye sensor switching to viewfinderNormal. To lock the screen: Setup → Finder/Monitor → Sel. Finder/Monitor → Monitor.
    Viewfinder looks blurryDioptre wheel set for someone elseTurn the small wheel beside the EVF until the text is crisp.
    Overheat iconLong 4K recording in heatSetup → Power Setting Option → Auto Power OFF Temp. → High. Flip the screen out, keep out of direct sun.
    Battery drains fastWi-Fi/Bluetooth, bright screen, IBIS on tripodAirplane mode on when not transferring. Carry a spare NP-FZ100.
    Recording stops by itselfCard too slowUse V60/V90 SD or CFexpress Type A for 4K 50p / S-I.

    Battery, cards & care

    Battery

    • NP-FZ100: roughly 520 shots (viewfinder) / 580 (screen), or around 1.5–2 hours of video.
    • Charges in-camera over USB-C; a USB-PD phone charger is fastest.
    • Can run from a power bank while filming long events.
    • Buy genuine Sony or reputable brands. Cheap copies swell and report wrong charge.

    Memory cards

    • Photos + 4K 25p: SD UHS-II V60 is plenty.
    • 4K 50p, S-I, long bursts: V90 SD or CFexpress Type A (slot 1 only).
    • Record to both slots for weddings: Shooting → Media → Rec. Media Settings → Simult. Rec.
    • Copy files off and format in-camera before every big shoot.

    Care in Indian weather

    • Monsoon: keep the camera and lenses in a dry box or airtight box with silica gel packets to prevent fungus.
    • AC to humid outside: leave the camera in its bag for 15–20 min so the lens doesn't fog.
    • Dust: change lenses facing down, quickly, out of the wind. Use a blower before a brush.
    • Never leave it in a hot car.
